## User operations and responsibilities

KafkaLume can view data, send or replay messages, create or delete topics, modify topic settings, increase partition counts, adjust consumer offsets, and perform cluster migrations. These operations may change business data, consumer progress, or cluster state. Confirm your permissions, target cluster, and the impact of each operation. For important environments, back up data and validate operations in a test environment first.
